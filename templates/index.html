ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built to support a closer look.

LivestockCare is a student project that screens cattle photos across six trained classes: lumpy skin disease, foot-and-mouth disease, IBK, mastitis, ringworm, and healthy.

Some conditions affect the eye, udder, mouth, or feet. Use a photo that clearly shows the area of concern. A model result is not a diagnosis or treatment plan.

THREE SIMPLE STEPS

How it works

01

Choose a photo

Upload a clear JPG or PNG image showing the animal or affected area.

02

Run the model

The trained model compares the photo with its six learned classes.

03

Review next steps

See the estimated class, confidence, and general precautions saved with the result.

i

For animal health concerns: contact a veterinarian. The model can be wrong, especially with unclear photos or conditions outside its training classes.
